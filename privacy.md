# Kotoba Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 24 September 2026

Kotoba is a vocabulary app and dictionary with Lock Screen and Home Screen widgets. **Kotoba does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data.** It has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics, no crash-reporting services and no tracking of any kind.

## Data stored on your device

Your favorites, the words you mark as known, your word history and your settings are stored only on your iPhone, in storage shared between the app and its widgets. They are never sent to us or to anyone else. They are included in your own device backups (iCloud or computer backups), which you control, and they are deleted when you delete the app.

## Network use

Kotoba works fully offline. It uses the network only when **you choose to download an optional dictionary pack** in Settings → Language Packs. The pack is downloaded from GitHub Releases, which GitHub, Inc. operates. As with any download, GitHub receives your device's IP address and standard technical information about the request, and handles it under the [GitHub Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). Kotoba sends no identifiers or personal information with this request.

Links on the Attributions screen open in Safari and are subject to the privacy policies of those websites.

## Apple features

- **Pronunciation** uses Apple's built-in text-to-speech, which runs on your iPhone.
- **Explain and Grammar Breakdown** (on devices with Apple Intelligence) use Apple's on-device language model. The text you select is processed on your iPhone and is not sent to us.

## Children

Kotoba is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal data from anyone, including children.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

## Contact

Questions about this policy: [open an issue](https://github.com/jiaweisun-github/kotoba-packs/issues) on the Kotoba packs repository.
